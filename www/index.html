Everything you need to run your programme

From building your roster to filling last-minute gaps — VolunteerFlow covers the full coordination workflow.

📅

Shift Scheduling

Monthly calendar view. Create shifts with date, time, location, and capacity limits in seconds. Publish or keep as draft until you're ready.

👥

Volunteer Roster

Import your entire list from CSV, track availability by day of week, and keep contact details current — all in one searchable table.

⚡

Dispatch Board

See who's available versus assigned at a glance. Assign or remove volunteers from a shift with a single tap — on desktop or tablet.

📱

SMS Notifications

Plug in your own SMS provider and send shift reminders or coverage requests directly to volunteers. Bring your own Twilio account — you stay in control of your messaging costs.

📊

Hours Reports

Generate shareable volunteer hours summaries with a single link. Recipients don't need an account — perfect for grant reporting.

📲

Mobile App

Full-featured iOS & Android app with a tablet-optimised dispatch board. Manage shifts and assignments from anywhere, on any device.
